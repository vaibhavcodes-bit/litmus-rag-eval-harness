TECHNICAL HIRING PROCESS

Resume Screening

During resume screening, recruiters evaluate whether a candidate's experience, skills, and qualifications match the requirements of the role.

Technical Interview

The technical interview evaluates technical knowledge, problem-solving ability, coding skills, system design knowledge, and the candidate's ability to explain technical decisions.

Behavioral Interview

The behavioral interview evaluates communication, teamwork, leadership, conflict resolution, adaptability, and how the candidate handled situations in previous roles.

Technical vs Behavioral Interviews

Technical interviews focus primarily on technical competence and problem solving. Behavioral interviews focus primarily on interpersonal skills, communication, teamwork, and past behavior.

Companies may use both interview types because technical competence alone does not guarantee that a candidate will communicate effectively or work well with a team.

System Design Interview

A system design interview evaluates a candidate's ability to clarify requirements, identify constraints, estimate scale, design system components, explain trade-offs, and discuss reliability and scalability.

Candidates should not immediately start writing code when given a system design question. They should first clarify requirements and constraints, discuss assumptions, estimate scale when appropriate, and outline the architecture before discussing implementation details.

Interview Follow-Up

Candidates should send a professional follow-up message after an interview. Ideally, the message should be sent within 24 hours.

The follow-up should thank the interviewer, mention something specific discussed during the interview, reinforce interest in the role, and briefly highlight relevant qualifications.
