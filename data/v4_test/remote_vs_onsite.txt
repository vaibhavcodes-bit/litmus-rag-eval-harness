REMOTE VS ON-SITE WORK POLICY

Remote Work

Remote work allows employees to work from an approved location outside the company's office. The primary benefits of remote work are flexibility, reduced commuting, and the ability to structure the work environment around individual productivity.

Remote employees are expected to maintain reliable internet connectivity, attend scheduled virtual meetings, communicate regularly with their teams, and complete assigned deliverables.

Remote employees are evaluated primarily on the quality and timeliness of their deliverables, communication, meeting participation, and ability to work independently.

On-Site Work

On-site work requires employees to work from the company's designated office location during established working hours. The primary benefits of on-site work are direct collaboration, immediate access to office resources, and easier face-to-face communication.

On-site employees are expected to maintain regular office hours, participate in in-person meetings, collaborate with colleagues, and follow office procedures.

On-site employees are evaluated based on the quality and timeliness of their deliverables, collaboration, attendance, participation in team activities, and communication with colleagues.

Remote vs On-Site Comparison

Remote work emphasizes flexibility, reduced commuting, and independent work. On-site work emphasizes direct collaboration, face-to-face communication, and access to office resources.

Both remote and on-site employees are evaluated on their deliverables and communication. However, remote evaluation places greater emphasis on independent execution and virtual communication, while on-site evaluation places greater emphasis on attendance, collaboration, and participation in office activities.
